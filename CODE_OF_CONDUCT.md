# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps this community:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair
corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. They may
remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not
align with this Code of Conduct.

Abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to
**nt.savinda@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.
